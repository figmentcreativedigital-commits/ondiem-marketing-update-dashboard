What shipped

Updates this period

Three items in flight. One of them produced the only conversion number in this report.

Converting

August $50 gift-card promo

Seven sends to 837 practices across six cities plus an all-cities list. The offer redeems on a promo code entered at shift creation. The codes have produced 20 filled shifts so far. The campaign runs to Aug 31 and the second reminder hasn't gone out yet, so there's more to come.

In progress

Partner site widget

Logan and Darya's partner availability widget detects any non-ondiem.com domain and renders “Find staff ready to work, powered by onDiem.” No tagged traffic yet. The CTAs need UTMs before we can separate widget sessions from existing referral traffic.

Launched

DOMA 2026

A six-email practice sequence launched Aug 6, running to Sep 29 — follow-up, fast signup, staffing network, ADA member, reliable coverage, pro calendar. All assets built. 44 sends, 7 opens. Not enough volume to read yet. First real numbers next period.

Core marketplace actions, week over week

Five of six rose. professional_accepted_offer is the most reliable booking proxy in the platform data.

Where the decline sits

Desktop fell 20.2%, mobile rose 9.8%. Mobile share went from 32.4% to 39.6% in a week.

Traffic sources

10,292 sessions in total.

Fewer people, doing more

Active users fell 10.4% while sessions rose 2.9%, and every core action except shift requests went up. Accepted offers rose 36.4% in the same week the promo filled 20 shifts. The two line up, though platform data can't confirm the connection on its own.

Concentration tightened again. Listings rose 5.6% while the practices creating them fell from 255 to 224 — 2.51 listings each up to 3.02. The pro side moved the other way: shift requests fell 14.1% while the pros making them rose from 97 to 120.

Two things to check. A (not set) source carried 1,156 sessions, 11.2% of platform traffic, with nothing like it the week before. Worth asking whether a tag changed. And GA4 credits google/cpc with 1,640 sessions against 385 recorded ad clicks — a factor of 4.3, close to the 4.4 last period. Sessions keep their original source across return visits, so paid's share reads high.

Completion follows the prompt

No SMS went out between Jul 20 and this pull, so this measures organic behaviour with no nudge. Eleven pros completed a section, against 45 cumulatively since the baseline. When the prompt stops, movement stops. Same pattern as the email drip. The fix is cadence, not copy.

The tracked cohort changed. This covers the 555 pros present in all three pulls (Jul 13, Jul 20, Aug 6). The last report tracked 765 on a different definition, so the two counts aren't comparable. Profile Photo is the strongest converter at 28 cumulative completions. Personal Bio is the biggest gap and barely moves.

137 pros are missing four of six sections. That group hasn't responded to anything so far — probably a channel problem rather than a message problem. Oregon (132) and Minnesota (60) lead by volume, the same two markets where the gift-card promo worked. New York and Virginia have the highest incompletion rates.

The round-one and round-two SMS clicks from last period (152 and 89) still have no completion counts attached.

What this means for next period

  • The promo converted. It's the only thing here that did. 837 emails produced 5 clicks and 20 filled shifts. Redemption ran four times the click count, so run this campaign on redemption. The spring mailer has also settled at 48 booked shifts rather than the 15 we reported while timecards were clearing.
  • The funnel improved while traffic thinned. Active users fell 10.4% and first visits 13.9%, but sessions rose, accepted offers rose 36.4%, and five of six core actions went up. Read with the promo, that's conversion moving the right way for the first time. It's also the first time the top of the funnel hasn't.
  • Discovery is going the other direction. AI visibility hit a new low at 26.7, onDiem is the only tracked brand losing ground, and competitor-owned pages are now the largest citation source at 37.5%. Princess Dental Staffing turns up in five separate places in this report.
  • The site still leaks at the same point. Form starts nearly doubled to 63 and produced one submission. The rate hasn't changed; the loss is bigger because more people are trying. /contact-us views rose 62% in the same week.
  • Three tracking items carry over. Paid has recorded zero conversions for four periods and lands on hub.ondiem.com, outside both GA4 properties. The partner widget still has no UTMs. And 11.2% of platform sessions arrived unattributed this week, which is new.
  • Two things to settle before the next report. Whether the August ADA email is scheduled or skipped, since its absence is the entire link-tracking story. And the RDH sign-up count, since 237 contacts are still in a workflow marked complete.
