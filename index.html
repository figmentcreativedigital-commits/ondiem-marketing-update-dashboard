Shipped

Schema on the core pages

Structured data added to the home, professionals and practices pages. This is what search engines and AI assistants read to work out what onDiem is and who it serves. It sits alongside last period's sitemap indexing fix, which shows up in the website section.

Working

Google review automation

18 reviews at 4.6★, up from 16 at 4.3 last period and 9 at 3.7 in early July. This is an automated ask, not organic drift. Built by Angie Trogstad with Dee Lopez, it now runs on closed email tickets as well as live chat. Before it, onDiem had not had a review in about a year.

Counts are the first batch, sent Jul 2–27. Nine reviews came in over the same stretch, more than the 5 recorded click-throughs, so the email-ticket version of the ask is likely contributing volume we are not measuring separately. No bounces, no unsubscribes, no spam reports across the 46 sends. Review totals captured Aug 20, so they are current state rather than a windowed number.

Which onDiem pages get cited

496 citations across 18 owned pages, Jul 23 – Aug 20.

onDiem fell 13.4 points over the four weeks. GoTu fell 10.4. Toothio and Kwikly also declined and Cloud Dentistry was flat, so the pattern from earlier periods — onDiem the only brand losing ground — no longer holds.

Perplexity is the engine that moved. 30.0 to 11.4 over four weeks while ChatGPT held flat at 32.9. One engine is dropping onDiem specifically.

The owned citation rate is the one moving the right way. onDiem's own domain went 6.0 to 6.6 and sits third of eight. Brand mention rate did not move: 8.8% against GoTu at 24.1%.

hub.ondiem.com is 30.4% of onDiem's AI citations — 151 across 15 pages, behind the homepage at 44.8%. The Darby hub page is the second strongest asset after the homepage. The paid search plan retires hub behind a redirect. Preserving those URLs matters here as much as it does in search.

A fifth week beginning Aug 16 reads 44.0 for onDiem, but on under a third of the usual sample, so it is excluded. It becomes a complete data point next period.

Aug 12–18, 2026

Wins, and what is on deck

  • The promo converted. 23 filled shifts, 10 worked and approved, $500 issued. Redemption is the number that matters here, not opens or clicks, and it is the number to run this campaign on.
  • The Darby widget is live and being used. 97 Darby-referred users in its first week, filtering at 14.4% against 7.9% for everyone else on the property. Over thirty days that gap is 18.5% against 7.0%.
  • The review automation is running on its own. Nine reviews and nearly a full star since early July, off a workflow nobody has to remember to run. It is the only third-party signal in this report moving up, and it counts for AI answers as much as for search.
  • The indexing fix landed. /practices went 121 to 253 and /shifts 46 to 123. Organic search is now 51.8% of site views and the site has risen three weeks running.
  • Discovery steadied. onDiem's four-week decline is now matched by three of five tracked competitors, and the owned citation rate rose from 6.0 to 6.6. Schema on the core pages should show up here next.
  • On deck: the signup event. Once it ships, paid search can record a conversion for the first time and the $1,536 becomes measurable. The widget's ‘show more’ events need the same treatment before we can report modal engagement.
  • Also on deck: the promo runs to Aug 31 with a second reminder still to go, and the partial AI visibility week beginning Aug 16 becomes a complete data point next period.
