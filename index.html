Updates this period

Three items landed at or just after the close of the window. None carry performance data yet; each is noted here so next period’s numbers have context.

In progress

Partner site widget

Logan and Darya have built the partner availability widget. It detects any non-ondiem.com domain and renders “Find staff ready to work, powered by onDiem.” Scope was kept narrow to move quickly; messaging can expand later. Preview and documentation are circulating. No performance data this period.

In market

August $50 gift-card promo

Six city-targeted sends went out Aug 5 to 701 practices in Portland, Minneapolis, Chicago, Atlanta, Houston and Miami. Practices earn a $50 gift card per shift posted and worked through Aug 31. The offer redeems on a promo code entered at shift creation, so clicks understate response — redemption is the measure.

Working

Google review automation

A happy CSAT response now triggers an automated review request. Reviews moved from 9 at 3.7 to 16 at 4.3 in about two months, after roughly a year with none. The request has been extended from live chat to email tickets. The app store listing sits separately at 5 reviews and 1.8.

Auction insights — impression share on brand terms

onDiem holds 64.7% impression share and 77.2% absolute top-of-page on its own name. Bars marked 10% are reported by Google as “< 10%”.

Two corrections worth carrying forward

Auction insights were read inverted last period. In Google Ads, outranking share on a competitor row means how often onDiem ranked above them. onDiem outranks Teero 59.4% of the time and Teero appears above onDiem 18.9% of the time when both show. The brand position is strong, not under pressure. The competitor set has however doubled from three to six.

Paid’s share of platform traffic is overstated. Platform GA4 credits google/cpc with 7,321 sessions; Google Ads recorded 1,667 clicks — a factor of 4.4. GA4 sessions carry their original source across return visits, so much of that 16.5% is returning users who first arrived via an ad. Separately, 1,567 of 1,615 attributed clicks land on hub.ondiem.com, which sits outside both GA4 properties in this report.

Reviews are moving. Visibility is not — yet.

The automated review request has taken Google from 9 reviews at 3.7 to 16 at 4.3 after roughly a year of none, and review sites account for 6.8% of citations. Those two facts sit next to each other but should not yet be joined: onDiem’s visibility fell from 43.3 to 36.0 over the same weeks. Two months of review activity cannot move a citation base this quickly. Treat reviews as a position being rebuilt, not as a lever already pulled.

Two open items affect this section directly. The app store listing sits at 5 reviews and 1.8 and the automation does not currently point at it. And two Google Business accounts remain unmerged, which splits the review count.

TAKEAWAYS

What this means for next period

  • Reach improved almost everywhere. Every handoff held flat or leaked. Instagram views roughly tripled with 81% from non-followers, onDiem appears across three AI engines, and paid holds 64.7% impression share on its own name. Against that: 230 form starts produced 5 submissions, 51 social posts produced 46 site visits, AI visibility produced 7, and a 451-lead event produced 4 sign-ups.
  • The one thing that converted was the gift-card mailer. 15 confirmed shifts from roughly eight practices, all in Portland and Minneapolis, driven by repeat posting from accounts that already existed. The August digital version targets those two markets among six.
  • Fix the forms before adding more traffic to them. The 2% completion rate is a known defect already logged behind the pro app. Every channel in this report routes to a site whose only conversion action is broken.
  • Give the August promo a measurable outcome. City-suffixed promo codes and a single UTM medium would let the next report attribute booked shifts to a market. Neither change costs anything and both must happen before the next send.
  • Close the measurement gap at hub.ondiem.com. It receives the paid traffic and the AI citations and sits outside both GA4 properties. Platform GA4 also credits paid with 4.4× the sessions Google Ads recorded.
  • Watch Princess Dental Staffing. Three separate appearances this period — 16% Instagram growth, new to the brand-term auction, and AI visibility from 3.3 to 22.
