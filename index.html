WEEKLY TAKEAWAYS

What this means for next week

  • Time everything to Monday. The marketplace and paid demand both peak Sunday-night into Monday–Tuesday mornings — schedule shift-supply pushes, emails and ad budget there.
  • Lean into reels and LinkedIn documents. Reels drove 58% of Instagram views from a fraction of the posts, and LinkedIn document/carousel posts converted at 25%+ into clicks. That's the content mix to scale.
  • Close the social/email → site gap. A large social effort produced just 52 website visits and email 12. The mobile email CTA and social link-in-bio paths are leaking the traffic they earn.
  • Coverage is the recurring pain. ‘Finding coverage’ is practices' softest survey area and the supply answer is more hygienists — exactly the 362 warm RDH leads (MD/VA/PA) and the 212 incomplete pro profiles worth activating.
  • Protect the brand in paid search. Teero outranks onDiem in 55.7% of shared brand auctions — and the same rivals (GoTu, Cloud) are the platforms pros multi-home on — while 0 conversions are tracked. Fix conversion measurement before reopening prospecting.
  • Answer ‘when do I get paid?’. Pay is the #1 question in 663 first-shift responses despite 91.6% satisfaction — a visible pay-timing explainer is a cheap retention win.
